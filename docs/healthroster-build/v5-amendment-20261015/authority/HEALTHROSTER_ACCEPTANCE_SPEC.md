For each applicable route instantiate current financial states **pending/not approved, approved-unpaid, part-paid, paid, protected, saved unissued frozen Draft, issued/unknown original transfer, held/recovery, invoiced-unpaid and paid+invoiced**. A state absent by native route construction is recorded as NATIVE_NOT_APPLICABLE with its exact native constraint proof; it is not silently skipped or called an unsupported move. Complete all permitted physical moves; a genuine unresolved financial/invoice dependency returns its exact review state and action instead of a fabricated zero or a permanent unsupported box.

Before and after **every** move/no-pay case capture these assertion sets:

* `P`: same genuine Candidate/actual client; all actual work/claim dates covered by B; exactly one direct `family_root_id` and one current placement; each old/current/carrier/correction alias resolves directly to it; no extra real claim/booking/submission/approval/signature; A History links B and B links A; original evidence IDs, versions and raw hashes equal.
* `S`: sorted exact Source invoice/correction certificate: accepted report member/row/lineage IDs and hashes; genuine work-event/movement IDs/economic hashes; window/manifest/presentation/binding IDs, invoice ID/status/number, physical invoice line IDs/counts, source net/VAT/gross per component/line/header, credit relationships, source expense/mileage money, issued document/support version and hashes. Existing Source certificate is equal before/after. For accepted uninvoiced Source, immutable accepted basis/allocation and eligibility are equal before/after; future actual invoice admits once through original A. B is excluded from ordinary billing. For `NO_FINAL_REPORT_YET`, no final money/member/line/invoice identity is invented.
* `M`: actual native Banking prior payments, realised recoveries and current frozen reservations attributed once to the same real work/components; no A-zero command plus independent full B offer; one initial move command; at most one continuation per accepted cut; partial pages cannot publish half a family. Original frozen cash/channel/client/rates/identity are equal. Unknown is NULL/review, never zero. Policy X post-Draft reads only frozen artifacts; TS_DAY remains YYYY-MM-DD.
* `R`: Source/Timesheet report JSON, CSV, print, invoice render/document/attachment, Office summary, Candidate page/detail/history and Banking stage/history all use the same original/current association; totals and row counts cannot double through an alias or a second carrier. Unqualified amounts remain explicitly pending.

Any failed invariant is FAIL and holds exposure. A physical COMPLETE is not a financial APPLIED or cash PAID result. A permitted financial-pending receipt passes only its physical/preservation assertions; its independent money-application case remains open until a real fixture outcome and continuation pass.
