# Earlier payment evidence: exact builder contract

This is a required part of Banking workorder §5 and Health workorder §§4/6. It closes SOL-12. It specifies new implementation, not an existing installed endpoint. The Office labels are **Review earlier payment**, **Check again** and **Use these payment records**. Internal names below belong in builder documentation only.

## 1. Purpose and limits

Moving an old Timesheet can succeed even when its old payment cannot yet be compared with the new rate. Preserve the move, old payment, existing Draft and Source invoice. Do not offer another full payment. Show the specific missing information and links to the original records. Other qualified work remains usable after the explicit review-only command has completed its native ordering barrier.

Use committed native records to derive the original worker's component, quantity, tax treatment, pay channel and comparable gross/source basis. An invoice, bank cash, a Timesheet paid flag, an uploaded spreadsheet or an Office-entered total cannot manufacture that basis. This contract adds no document-upload system and does not restart the retired Banking engine. A genuine earlier debt that is already qualified can use its existing Finance Case/manual-collection action; that cannot establish an unknown gross debt.
