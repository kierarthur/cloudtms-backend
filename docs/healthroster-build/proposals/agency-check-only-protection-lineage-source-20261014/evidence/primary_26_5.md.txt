### 26.5 The invoiceable Timesheet, per-shift pay and pay-only protection (owner rounds 12–15; R-207, R-212, R-218, R-222)

**Rebuilt for rounds 14–15. This replaces the earlier §26.5–26.5a: protection lives on T0's segment, R0/P0 for paid protections, and the two-step Amend of protected hours.**

**1. T0 holds finalised HealthRoster shifts only (round 15 item 2; R-222).**
- The worker's signed MyTMS submission is the **claim**. Its immutable submission and the comparison rows (`weekly_timesheet_source_comparisons`, §26.3) keep every claimed shift for the queries.
- **The invoiceable Timesheet T0 carries only shifts that HealthRoster has finalised, at the finalised times.** An unfinalised shift, protected or not, is never on T0. There is nothing to remove and no "deemed not worked" step for an unprotected shift; the round-14 deeming idea is superseded. "Deemed not worked" now means only the consequence of Office's Unprotect of a still-waiting protected shift (item 3; round 15 reconciliation item 4).
- **How T0 is aligned (REUSE, no new edit path; round-6 M7: ONE Office action).** Every preset T0 change runs as **one Office action** through a single thin owner, `public.weekly_source_agency_timesheet_change_v1(p_contract_week_id, p_reason, p_expected_signature, p_request_key, p_actor_user_id)`, replay-safe by request key. It resolves the current T0 fresh from the Contract week and refuses a rotated id (item 13). Its request row is also the store for the return hold of item 5. The action covers:
  - Authorise;
  - "Shift now finalised · add to the Timesheet";
  - **"Shift now matches HealthRoster · Authorise"** (critic round 8 §8 item 2): a held, disputed shift on an authorised T0 whose HealthRoster hours later match. It is Office's re-authorise, which clears that segment's `exclude_from_pay`;
  - "Accept HealthRoster hours";
  - the same-draft round trip as **one click** (critic round 8 §8 item 1; item 5).

  The equal-hours case (a protected shift finalised at exactly the protected hours, £0 netting) **still needs Office's re-authorise click**. The netting and the clearing are automatic, the authorisation is not (round-12 final decision; critic round 8 §8 item 4). The Worker request runs the steps below in sequence:
  1. While T0 is unauthorised and on no invoice, T0's schedule is set to the finalised shifts through the existing edit owner `contract_week_manual_upsert_atomic` (`03102026_0500…:10`).
  2. The existing Worker recompute runs (`enqueue_ts_financials` → `tsfin_write_current_snapshot_single_bounded`).
  3. When the TSFIN is fresh (the NEXT publisher's own test, `10102026_1236…:415-425`), the authorise wrapper of item 4 completes the authorisation. The recorded actor is the Office user who clicked.

  The click is the authorisation (R-082). The Worker runs the recompute inline for this one Timesheet before calling the wrapper, so the user sees one click and one result. If the recompute is not fresh in time, the action returns `AGENCY_INVOICE_TSFIN_NOT_READY` ("Still recalculating — try again in a moment"); this refusal is only a safety net (AM-1). A replay of the same request key returns the stored result. Nothing authorises without an Office click.

**2. Pay per shift.**

| Shift | On T0? | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| finalised and matching | yes | paid from T0 |
| finalised, worker disputes the hours (a query raised before authorisation; after authorisation the worker cannot query, round 17 item 3), unprotected | yes, at HealthRoster's hours | held: T0 segment `exclude_from_pay=true` |
| unfinalised or missing, unprotected | **no** | not paid |
| **protected** (unfinalised, missing or disputed) | only if finalised (then segment `exclude_from_pay=true`) | **pay-only protected pay at the worker's submitted hours** (item 3). A protected shift that is still waiting **holds the week's invoice** (§26.4 condition 8). So does a protected shift that HealthRoster has finalised at **different** hours, until Office chooses "Accept HealthRoster hours" (round 17 item 4) |
| HealthRoster higher than the claim (R-209, "! No hold") | yes, at HealthRoster's hours | paid |

The invoice still waits for the complete matching week (§26.4). Protected hours never appear on the invoice; but a protected shift that is still waiting, or finalised at different hours, holds the week's invoice (§26.4 condition 8; owner round 15 reconciliation item 4, round 17 item 4).

**3. Protected pay is PAY-ONLY (round 14 item 2; round 15 items 2 and 7; R-207, R-218).**
- **The protection store** (`private.weekly_source_check_only_shift_protections`, NEW-JUSTIFIED store only) holds the worker's submitted hours, state and audit, keyed by the week and the shift's `work_event_id` (item 13), never by a Timesheet id. It is the **source** of a protected-pay position.
- **The protected-pay position** sits on T0's own NEXT pay record. NEXT's existing `PROTECTED_WORK` position family is extended to read this store (WT `10102026_1236…:2523-2674`). It is a Banking Pay request: **NX-AI-6**, with the exact contract in the handoff. No Banking Pay file is edited here.
- **Protect, Amend and Unprotect change pay by the difference only:**
  - no T0 edit, no unauthorise, no R0/P0 and no invoice line effect (Unprotect of a still-waiting shift does release the week's invoice hold, below);
  - Amend and Unprotect after payment record an overpayment of the difference only, never refused (R-213);
  - an increase is paid at the next run.

  The earlier two-step Amend of protected hours is withdrawn; R-218 is rewritten to the pay-only rule.
- **Unprotect** (round 15 reconciliation item 4) is offered **only while the shift is still waiting**. A finalised shift at the protected hours clears automatically once it is on T0 and T0 is authorised (R-166; item 6, §26.5b). At different hours, only Accept or Wait is offered (R-227, below).
  - **Shift still waiting:** it is **deemed not worked**. The position is reconciled to zero, and anything already paid becomes an overpayment of the difference (never refused, R-213). The week's invoice is released.

  Leaving the shift protected keeps it waiting.
- **A protected shift finalised at DIFFERENT hours (owner round 17 item 4; R-227)** has exactly two resolutions:
  - **Accept HealthRoster hours.** One Office action (item 1) settles the shift on T0 at HealthRoster's hours. There are two timings. If the shift was finalised **before** T0's alignment, it is already on T0 with `exclude_from_pay=true`, and Accept releases it and nets. If it is finalised **after** authorisation, Accept adds it to T0. Either way condition 8 holds the invoice until then. The protection is reconciled to HealthRoster, and pay settles only the difference: an overpayment of the difference, or a top-up. The protection clears and the week can then be invoiced.
  - **Wait for a further import.** The protection is kept unchanged, and it **still holds the week's invoice** (§26.4 condition 8).

  There is **no** "keep the worker's hours" or keep-as-paid option, and no "invoice HealthRoster's hours and absorb the difference" option (critic option 2 rejected). If a later import finalises the shift at the protected hours, the normal netting applies (£0 extra).

- calls the **unchanged** `timesheet_authorise_generic_atomic`;
- runs the validation writer (§26.4).

If this is impossible, the build stops and reports, naming `17092026_0610…:1142-1160`. An Authorise before any export covers the week is refused unless a shift is protected; protected pay then flows through the position.

**6. A shift HealthRoster finalises later (round 15 items 2 and 7, and the owner-confirmed reconciliation; R-222).**
- **A protected shift finalised later is ALWAYS added to T0.** It is never put on a new Timesheet: the waiting protected shift held the week's invoice, so T0 cannot have been invoiced. T0 is changed through the item 5 route.
- **An unprotected shift finalised later before T0 is invoiced** is added to T0 the same way.
- **A new Timesheet is needed ONLY after issue (owner round 17 item 3 removes the post-authorisation query case).**
  - A new shift goes on a new additional Timesheet T1, created by the existing additional-week owner (`handleContractWeekCreateAdditionalWeeklyAdjustment`, `index.js:175235-175242`, route `:202055-202060`) and filled by `contract_week_manual_upsert_atomic`, then onto a new invoice through normal Batch Generate.
  - Amended or removed hours go through R0/P0 and a credit note for that Timesheet only (§26.6; R-240).
- **Never paid twice (round 15 item 7).** If the shift was protected, its pay on T0 at HealthRoster's hours is **netted against the protected pay already paid** for the same worker, work date and shift identity. Only the difference is settled:
  - HealthRoster higher → top-up;
  - HealthRoster lower → an overpayment of the difference (R-213);
  - equal → nothing.

  The protection is then cleared (`CLEARED`/`RECONCILED_BY_IMPORT`, audited) and the shift is invoiceable.
- **The netting key is the economic key** `candidate_id + work_date + work_event_id` (R-230; a finalised shift inherits the protection's `work_event_id`, item 13 (3)). It is never `booking_id`, `segment_id` or Request Id. In the normal case the protected position and the shift are both on T0's single NEXT work. **Netting is always cross-work by that key** (handoff NX-AI-6 item 5): a post-issue T1, R0/P0 (own works), and a T0 whose booking family changed (safeguard (b), item 13 (6)). Post-authorisation worker queries no longer exist (round 17 item 3). The netting is order-independent: while a protection is ACTIVE for a key, the key's payable amount is the protected value. The contract is in handoff NX-AI-6 item 5 (tests PX-1…PX-7, WH-1…WH-3).

**13. Protected-pay identity — survives every Timesheet rotation (`PROTECTED_PAY_IDENTITY_REVIEW.md` §F–§H; R-230).**

The preset's T0 can rotate to a new physical row. Rotation happens on rejection or resubmission, route change, QR action, and withdrawal followed by resubmission, and its segment ids change with its hours. So nothing in the plan is anchored to a physical Timesheet, segment, booking or Request Id.

**Actions** (shared predicate R-216; one person):

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Amend** (change protected hours) | position changed to the new hours; difference paid or recorded as an overpayment; T0 and the invoice untouched |

| **Review protected pay** | read-only details |

### 26.5c Late worker submissions, unlimited re-imports and when a week matches (owner rounds 13–14; R-219, R-220)

**Late submissions (R-219).**
- **The request stays answerable after OVERDUE** (QD `:183, 437, 1491-1521`). "Use HealthRoster hours" never closes it (QD `:690-713`).
- **Before authorisation.** One preset arm in the existing `_candidate_record_capabilities_v1` (`04092026_1603…:12, 230-235, 349-350`) lets the worker submit over a "Use HealthRoster hours" T0. Today that submission is refused with `CANDIDATE_RECORD_MUTATION_LOCKED` (controlling check `10102026_1347…:1045-1051`; the older `07082026_2120…:2408-2420` copy is superseded), because the Office edit leaves `PENDING_AUTH` (`index.js:59905-59908`). After the arm, the submission is compared again, and a mismatch reopens the query and Protect pay flow.
- **After authorisation.** The worker can no longer submit or query for T0's shifts. The existing authorised-record lock applies (§26.5 item 7; round 17 item 3). The earlier "admitted and held" branch and the "post-authorise query → new Timesheet" branch are both deleted.
- **After issue.** The worker stays locked (an issued T0 is authorised; owner round 17 item 3). A later change can come only from a HealthRoster import, through the post-issue route (§26.6).

**Re-imports (R-220).**
- Re-imports are unlimited.
- Each one writes fresh comparison rows and re-runs the alignment of an unauthorised T0 (§26.5 item 1) and the validation writer.

**When a week matches (R-220).** All of the following hold:
- every T0 shift is finalised and agrees with HealthRoster;
- no query raised before authorisation about a shift on T0 is open (a query about an unfinalised or missing, unprotected shift never counts, §26.4 condition 3);
- no protected shift is still waiting, and none is finalised at different hours unresolved (§26.4 condition 8);
- the export is ticked complete.

Agreement comes from a later import, or from Office's **Accept HealthRoster hours** (R-227). Unprotect of a still-waiting shift ends its protection (deemed not worked; it was never on T0) and releases the invoice hold. Only then is `VALIDATION_OK` written and the invoice prepared by normal Batch Generate.
