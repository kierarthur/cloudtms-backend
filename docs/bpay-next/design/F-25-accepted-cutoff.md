## Decision

Extend the existing SEAL_ENQUEUE transaction and Candidate command barrier. The PREPARE command's existing `agency_sequence` is the single cutoff. Preserve sealed 1590/0570 selection, approved revisions, lease/replay semantics, Candidate order, existing calculators and genuine money locks. At acceptance retain exact Candidate/policy/query/origin evidence; after earlier accepted Candidate commands drain, append the effective per-WORK cut. Later current pointers/settings cannot replace that evidence. Do not manufacture a second clock, Workbench cutoff, comparison engine or F-26 epoch treatment.

Two traps make a CLAIM/PIN-only patch incomplete:

1. PREPARE command members currently appear during background enrollment, whereas the required accepted receipt has a command-member FK and must exist at Enqueue. Materialize immutable selected member identities in Enqueue and make enrollment reuse/validate them. Do not create jobs, advance enrollment counters or mark the command fully enrolled early.
2. Downstream query-pin, Source bound-reader/I-1 assertion, capture/hold and outcome seed routines themselves require today's current pointers. Their accepted-origin successors must validate retained genuine publication/certificate/binding evidence while preserving actual hold/effect/book conservation. Removing only PIN's current-revision test leaves later rejections.

## Additive schema and exact identities

Use one new canonical migration `DDMMYYYY_HHMM_banking_pay_next_accepted_input_v1.sql`, followed by a named repeatable closure later than the current final definers. Do not edit frozen migration/core types or 0570. Reuse penny/domain types and the existing logical-owner-to-CURRENT_USER release adapter, private RLS/ACL pattern, no browser or service-role direct access.

Required tables:

- `private.bpay_next_accepted_input`: `id`, `run_id`, `prepare_command_id`, `candidate_id`, `module_epoch`, `accepted_sequence`; captured normalized channel, exact Umbrella/company identity and VAT treatment; pay-date/current-week identity; exact Candidate floor and finance-policy/rule evidence; original sealed case selection revision (or explicit no-case scope). UNIQUE `(prepare_command_id,candidate_id)`. Composite FK `(prepare_command_id,accepted_sequence)` to command; `(prepare_command_id,candidate_id)` to existing command_member; `(run_id,candidate_id)` to selection_candidate; same run_command/epoch guards. Immutable values, not a mutable run-level channel.
- `private.bpay_next_effective_work_cut`: UNIQUE `(accepted_input_id,work_id)` with same-Candidate FK to WORK. Retain original `(run_id,work_id)` manifest/choice and original expected revision; effective immutable revision; predecessor command/publication identity and sequence; exact selected approved-line/component/source-component/segment provenance; accepted Source receipt/head/I-1/binding version/book basis; valuation policy and immutable rate/line provenance; exact retained query evidence identity. An ALL selection retains its certified complete member count/identities, not a later scan of current lines. A SUBSET preserves the original chosen members and a certified exact lineage mapping if the effective earlier revision differs.

There is a timing distinction: the effective cut is appended after predecessor completion, but nonqueued query/channel/status inputs must already have immutable witnesses at Enqueue. Therefore the migration also needs the R2 per-WORK query/accepted-origin records: a small typed immutable accepted-WORK evidence child keyed `(accepted_input_id,work_id)` with exact origin/query/selected membership provenance, referenced by the later effective cut. It must not be an unbounded whole-Candidate history JSON blob. If selected case/component status or floor inputs have no immutable version owner, retain bounded typed accepted case/component evidence children under the same accepted_input, linked to exact sealed selection items. These are physical receipt children of the one cutoff, not another snapshot protocol.

Keep original selection/revision IDs distinct from effective IDs. Existing `bpay_next_selected_component` FKs bind immutable original choice+approved line; do not rewrite them or join them falsely to a successor line UUID. Only exact stable component/source-component/segment lineage can prove a mapping. A missing/ambiguous mapping refuses explicitly; date alone cannot establish identity.

Useful covering/FK indexes: accepted `(run_id,candidate_id)` and `(prepare_command_id,candidate_id)`; effective `(accepted_input_id,work_id)` plus referenced publication/revision IDs; per-work query/evidence exact accepted-input/WORK; selected case component/period lookups. Do not add speculative broad indexes or another sequence.

## Acceptance and ordered resolver

Acceptance transaction, inside existing SEAL_ENQUEUE:

1. Exact replay lookup and argument/sealed selection verification first. Retain original body/selection/request IDs; reject conflicting same-key payload. No side effects or new query check on retained acceptance.
2. For a fresh Source admission, take existing `weekly_source_pay_query_admit_v2()` before business locks, as its current contract requires. Inspect and retain exact selected Source query state/evidence. BLOCKED or UNAVAILABLE before the cutoff must truthfully refuse the affected Candidate without holds; never lazily treat missing evidence as CLEAR.
3. Read/lock only selected Candidate inputs and exact selected WORK/case/component/policy/query origins in a consistent existing owner order. Retain immutable version receipts and exact payload digests. Bind channel/company, complete rule/floor and case eligibility facts, including explicit empty/NOT_AFFECTED scope; do not let a later new case create a CaseSelectionRequired rejection for an accepted no-case Draft.
4. Allocate PREPARE using 1404, record exact command members and append accepted receipts/query children atomically, then retain current expected-member/enrollment semantics. The clock is last in the publisher's existing WORK→revision→clock lock chain. **Do not acquire agency clock and then lock WORK/CASE rows**, which would invert publication order. Audit participating writer order before implementation; hold field-owner locks until receipt/sequence commit.
5. An earlier publication is identifiable before application by its existing immutable command/publication/work/revision receipt. Record the greatest applicable accepted predecessor sequence below this PREPARE sequence for each selected WORK. Unknown/unversioned preceding inputs cannot be reconstructed later by timestamps: refuse truthfully before acceptance or add the exact participating version-receipt seam.

`private.bpay_next_seal_effective_work_cut_v1(p_job_id uuid,p_lease_nonce uuid,p_owner_epoch bigint,p_work_id uuid)` is private owner-only. Recommend returning the cut UUID (or one closed row contract), never caller-supplied money. Lock/verify current module NEXT; exact PREPARE command and Candidate; run/accepted_input identity; current lease nonce/owner epoch, deadline and job phase; selected WORK belongs to that same Candidate; accepted sequence equals job.command_sequence; no earlier unfinished Candidate job. Verify the chosen predecessor's genuine publication APPLIED/DONE and command completion, retained immutable revision/line evidence, selection completeness and captured origin/query proof. INSERT the cut once. Retry returns exactly that row after verifying immutable identity/digest; conflict refuses. No UPDATE to cut, choice or approved revision; no refreshed live query/origin and no obsolete-line reinterpretation.

The resolver may wait/retry for the known earlier command; it cannot silently select today's last revision. Later job receipts are excluded. Existing earlier-Candidate barrier remains in CLAIM and money writers. The accepted effective revision is the latest applicable command **below this PREPARE**, which can differ from the manifest's earlier original revision without mutating that manifest.

## Nonqueued producers and genuine price provenance

R2 explicitly requires nonqueued channel/company/floor/rule/query mutations to record immutable version evidence atomically in the same existing command order. Current receiver accepts a text command kind, but an added metadata-only kind must have explicit bounded enrollment handling; it cannot fall through to a financial job or strand the enrollment clock. Reuse existing exact journal/event/version receipts where present. If a producer lacks immutable before/after evidence, a timestamp or latest-row read is insufficient. Wire that owner or keep its affected acceptance typed-refused; do not claim F-25 whole closure while it remains unwired.

Ordinary case rule producers already present include 0330 case creation, 0410 case transfer post and the final 2018 work-collection branch (current rule pointer writes at 4199). Queued financial changes participate through their genuine commands; do not recast them as metadata or skip their barriers. Candidate/umbrella/query producers may be outside these NEXT owners: enumerate their exact writes during implementation and add only the narrow receipt seam required by §4, preserving invoice/Source authority. No generic trigger that journals unrelated agency writes.

Already fixed price evidence is not a gap: core1324 has immutable `bpay_next_work_revision.valuation_policy_id`, approved_line, shift/break/rate detail, per-revision `bpay_next_rate_schedule` and exact Source chosen-detail digest. No `active_price`-named NEXT release field/read was found. Record actual revision/approved-line/rate-schedule/policy/window/detail provenance; never invent an active-price ID, blend rate, repeat conversion or use current contract rates at delayed capture. Earlier accepted new rate approvals use their genuine revision/publication; later rates reach the next Draft.

## Captured origin versus live monetary ownership

Add narrowly scoped private captured-origin helpers (exact signatures must be recorded in inventory): accepted binding/I-1 proof, bound-source snapshot and Source disposition seed proof. Pass accepted cut identity or frozen-line provenance; do not let caller UUIDs bypass validation. Validate same Candidate/WORK/component/revision/approved-line, genuine immutable Source receipt or I-1 certificate digests, publication command lineage, selected basis and accepted sequence. These paths may survive a later authorised head/binding/book pointer, but cannot fabricate a receipt, change an approved amount, ignore a prior command, read an absent query as CLEAR or adopt comparison-only 0157/0158 types before their owning cards.

Separate current **pointer/currentness** from actual money: preserve Candidate/WORK/position/book/hold locks; nonnegative and defined basis checks; actual realised/held counters; exact one ACTIVE hold witness; genuine settled-effect/hold/transfer/member evidence; native book equality and revision/conservation; residual/capacity and same-channel restrictions. Accepted economic shares are immutable; actual held/realised money cannot be double spent. If a genuine external money change cannot be reconciled through accepted ordering, return a typed refusal, never reprice or erase guards. UNBOUND book seeding in both capture and outcome uses retained origin plus actual seed witnesses. Later head/query alone cannot invalidate accepted Draft inputs.

Case completion starts from **1958**, retaining its `BPAY_NEXT_COMPARISON_NOT_INSTALLED` Source refusal before initial/resumed capture writes, exact ordinary NOT_AFFECTED count/selection seals and GROSS_DEDUCT provenance. Captured rule/status/period/floor/balance basis controls instruction economics. Actual component/case/period holds remain checked under locks. Preserve the specific existing funded-rule successor and oldest-funded/created ordering; do not turn current_rule_id into an alternate policy amendment mechanism. WB-27 later owns the shared STABLE allocation plan; F-25 does not introduce a second calculator.
