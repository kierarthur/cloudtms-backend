BD124 operational ceiling: five failed PREPARE STEP attempts at one retained checkpoint. After failures1–4 the original job retries after30/60/120/240seconds through durable due discovery. Failure5 becomes Needs attention. Successful validated progress resets this consecutive failure count. Staff Try again resumes the same native identity and sealed economic inputs, advances the Candidate's execution preparation revision once before any future hold changes, and retains its captured economic revision and checkpoint. It does not recapture work, reprice, expand selection or invoke a new DR05 WORKER_REPREPARE job. Scope closure: the card's failed-Candidate Draft preparation is PREPARE only; other job kinds retain their existing owners/recovery and never acquire this ceiling or an inaccessible attention state. Existing definite POSITION_APPLY errors retain WB19's actual visible same-job recovery. Rewriting execution/settlement/Case money owners is outside this card.

## Safety and acceptance

Owner E4/E5 design correction: the new ceiling applies to the five economic preparation phases NEW/PINNING/RESERVE/ALLOCATE/FINANCE_ALLOCATE and the two postfinancial projection phases WORKBENCH_WORK/WORKBENCH_CASE. Exhausted economic preparation failures become worker REVIEW with a finite PF08_NEEDS_ATTENTION issue and native preparing→review counts, while the same job retains its exact unfinished phase/cursor. Final Review permits only this exact typed economic attention exception; Confirm still refuses it. Postfinancial projection attention retains its genuine READY/REVIEW/CANCELLED worker state and counts; it cannot falsely complete native Review until resumed projection is finished. The progress row exposes the genuine operational failure and same-job retry. Mandatory RELEASE/expiry/discard cleanup is excluded from this ordinary preparation ceiling and retains its existing bounded-page/native-expiry recovery; it never acquires a paused attention state. Staff same-job economic Retry advances only execution preparation_revision, restores the retained prior economic issue/count bucket, and resumes the checkpoint; captured economic revision stays unchanged. Projection Retry preserves its revision and bucket. WORKBENCH phases with a RUNNING RELEASE/PROJECT binding are mandatory cleanup and excluded from the ceiling before phase classification. All current execution associations must preserve the original DR05 lifecycle. The earlier proposed PREPARING/no-revision exception and blanket projection counter transition are rejected by independent review and are not built.

Unknown outcome closure: failure bookkeeping compares the exact invocation under native locks and retains any active lease. It may durably schedule backoff, but it must not publish actionable attention or change worker/root completion while the original lease remains active. Its fifth uncertain attempt keeps retry_count5 and due no earlier than lease expiry; a bounded reconciliation at native CLAIM admission under the same Run→Candidate→job order either observes committed progress/DONE and clears stale failure state, or promotes the unchanged expired checkpoint to typed attention. Original STEP replay is not disabled while its unknown lease remains active. No new attempt is claimed beyond the ceiling before that reconciliation. Successful advanced/DONE authority wins.
