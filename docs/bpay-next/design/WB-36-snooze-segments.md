# WB-36 design — exact snooze and segment attribution

Sequence48, 10 October 2026. Root read the full card/all19 rows, R2 section1 and all three legacy validate/upsert/clear bodies. Rule B, OR47 and master successor order bind this design. No app/hosted acceptance is claimed.

Exact named deliverables D08–D11: private snooze, append-only snooze event, single-use warning acknowledgement and named CHECK/CONTEXT versus ACK_ISSUE/SET/CLEAR separation. D14–D19: approved_segment, selected_segment, run_work_segment, immutable work_segment_origin_share with separate append-only realised-effect membership, record_work_segment_origin_shares_v1 and derived work_segment_gross_balance_v1. Small necessary support consists of private receipt/selection page state, child integrity guards, the worker-financial basis helper and existing shift_detail.segment_key. No new economic/payment unit or per-segment payroll NET is permitted.

Schema: approved_segment belongs to an immutable approved day line, carries the upstream Weekly segment_id or Source work_event_id, exact clocks/hours/five bucket hours/rates and approved gross. Its exact children reconcile to the parent at seal. Daily/aggregate has no fabricated segment. Selected_segment belongs to work_choice(run_id,work_id,expected_revision_id), including ALL mode, and records INCLUDED/SNOOZED; bounded pages seal exact membership before accepted Create. Run_work_segment freezes selected revision and approved identity plus realised/held gross credit, payable/recovery difference and excluded positive remainder. Removed historical segments retain their exact original day and negative difference; absent identities never attach by date/ordinal.

Boundedness: selection child pages contain at most100 exact segment identities and have immutable page receipts/counters; seal checks maintained counts. Capture uses a separate exact segment/parent cursor with at most100 attribution rows per call, retaining RESERVE until both position and segment child counters complete. A100-work/component page never implies an unlimited nested segment loop. Existing approved producer traversal is preserved; no unproved producer-count bound is asserted. Duplicate segment_key anywhere in a revision is refused before seal, never repaired with date/ordinal suffixes. Source retains its actual existing SOURCE:component parent, not a day-key rewrite.

Credit disposition: before any positive hold, exact attributed realised/ACTIVE-held child totals must equal each captured parent money total. Unexplained real prior gross credit yields named SEGMENT_CREDIT_ATTRIBUTION_UNAVAILABLE REVIEW; NULL/absence is not zero and no historical shares are seeded. Clean launch allows genuinely zero credit without conversion. A segment moved between dates keeps one run_work_segment identity plus the necessary typed parent-attribution child linked to each real run_position. Old parents retain their exact credit and negative difference; current parents carry their actual current approval and only their own credit. No old-day credit is moved to a new day. Multiple prior moves use actual child membership rather than fixed old/new columns. Positive and negative segment differences within one parent remain distinct; the specified signed recovery origin owner must consume actual negative origins, never hide them through snooze or assign their credit to an included sibling.

Origin shares belong simultaneously to the exact run-work segment, run line and hold. Hold membership is immutable. A separate effect-membership child appends realisation/collection origins; it never mutates the original hold share to attach an effect. Derived balances read indexed exact work/day/segment facts and parent ACTIVE/REALISED/RELEASED status, never Candidate history. Child reserved/source and target ex/VAT/inclusive sums must equal their existing day parent; allocation uses the actual gross valuer and deterministic cumulative rounding, never gross ratios to allocate PAYE NET. Returns/reissues preserve realised gross and original cash membership, with no new wage/hold.

Snooze: typed WHOLE_TIMESHEET/SEGMENT/EXPENSE_LINE/EXPENSES_ALL/CASE with exact Candidate/work/revision/physical and segment/component/case identity. One active scope, persisted version, until_date inclusive in Europe/London, note maximum500, native closed kind/aliases, immutable request receipt and audit. Whole/segment mutual exclusion is rechecked under existing Candidate-then-work order. Non-segment work gets a named refusal. BLOCKED_TIMESHEET excludes the exact approved chosen shift, never its same-day siblings. Whole scope covers genuine approved Timesheet members; expense scopes cover actual expense/mileage members only. CASE never rewrites case due/pause/schedule.

The required worker-pay basis includes approved source identity/channel/tax, quantities, worker pay and exact authorised worker control evidence; retains a genuine captured company where the native owner supplies it, without requiring an absent Source-company field. It excludes charge-only amounts, physical/TF revision identifiers and approval timestamps that can change without worker-pay change. Each selected exact scope uses sorted stable identities. Genuine changed/removed membership cancels stale controls with append-only audit through WB44; unchanged worker basis preserves them across charge-only revisions. SNOOZE_SEGMENT_STALE excludes nothing. Position/earned/frozen facts never change from snoozing.

Finite basis operands are native work identity, stable segment/expense/component identity, source channel and any genuinely captured company, tax treatment, authorised exclusion, quantity/five worker hours, exact used worker rates and approved worker gross; CASE additionally uses its actual worker-facing type/component/rule quantity/value. Full Source HEAD/book/binding IDs, component/inventory/calculation digests, whole policy/settings/rate-schedule JSON, Client charge and display order/labels are provenance outside this worker basis. A separate expected immutable revision/control version enforces concurrency. Worker-relevant source economic identity or authorised exclusion changing really changes the basis.

Concrete child order/counters: selection pages sort native segment_key and record page_no, first member number, item_count and exact request body; work_choice maintains segment_page_count/segment_count/segment_sealed_count. Seal compares exact identities with the immutable expected_revision approved inventory: ALL contains every genuine approved segment, SUBSET every segment of the chosen components. Foreign, duplicate or omitted identities refuse; caller-supplied counters cannot establish completeness. Removed historical negative attribution is retained separately. Accepted dispositions are immutable. Capture orders (work_id,segment_key,component_key) and maintains a job child cursor plus parent-attribution expected/processed counters. Each call admits at most100 child attributions and cannot advance ALLOCATE until the expected/processed counts reconcile. The original run_position contains the conserved locked day snapshot before child capture; each completed child's exact realised/held aggregate must reconcile that snapshot before its parent becomes holdable. The R2-named two-argument share recorder writes one completed parent's exact frozen membership atomically inside1620, with indexed native parent count/sum validation and no live/history traversal. It never publishes a partial parent or introduces a new hold lifecycle/protocol. The derived gross reader uses exact indexed native work/segment/parent keys and existing owner-maintained parent statuses; there is no agency/Candidate history traversal.

CHECK/CONTEXT is a bounded read with existing active-admin/service/NEXT authority, exact supported scope/version/basis/conflicts and official-date warnings. It issues no token or receipt and does not prune. ACK_ISSUE alone writes a30-minute actor/module/scope/version/basis/date/warning-bound token. SET checks exact request replay first, then current scope/version/basis/conflicts and consumes the matching token once in its successful transaction. Dated PRE_SAVE always warns before/includes next official pay run. PRE_OPEN warns only for genuine resolved rates. Missing/stale tokens refuse. Indefinite wins is reported from actual covering scopes. CLEAR uses expected_version and retains financial facts.

Rule-B freeze uses existing expected_revision_id and captured selection objects only. Append/segment pages capture exact offered control membership; accepted seal does not recalculate later live snoozes. PIN keeps its existing post-click revision REVIEW and query snapshot logic. Capture reads sealed membership and actual locked money credit; hold reserves only included positive differences. Negative differences remain visible to the existing recovery owner regardless of snooze. No F25 resolver, producer version protocol or new public-table trigger.
