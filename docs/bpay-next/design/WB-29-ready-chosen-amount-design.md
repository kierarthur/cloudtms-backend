## Binding scope

VAT and deductions come in Draft. The existing list reader supplies that amount from the saved WB29/WB45 decision. This replaces before-pay-date valuation **only for resolved decisions**. Pay-method changes after accepted Create are not newer information under OR46.

Thus no general conversion of every Ready row is authorized here. No decision retains the original authorised Source display. A qualified saved decision changes the Ready **display** to its chosen Target ex VAT. All Source A/R/H/residual/reservation fields remain literal Source money. Draft/accepted amounts remain frozen native results. The card requires one shared quote, independent bucket identity and capture precedence (02_WORKBENCH:1081,1089–1094,1117–1119), not browser arithmetic or a new pricing owner.
