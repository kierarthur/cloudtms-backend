M : 0) - B` directly to Candidate S is correct because native line units are disjoint. **A separate Work baseline witness is not required for a component-only edit.** My provisional suggestion that it was required was stronger than necessary; the investigator's proposed Work-by-Work calculation and this direct sum are mathematically equivalent.
