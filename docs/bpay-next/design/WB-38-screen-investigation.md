## Shared form and result requirements

- **Create:** small native window, a factual subject line and relevant Candidate/Client/shift context, Snooze indefinitely unticked, a blank date, UK date presentation, an earliest-date hint from today's authoritative London business date, optional note and one Apply snooze primary action.
- **Amend:** active snooze only, saved date/indefinite state and saved note, title Amend snooze, Save changes primary, and the separate destructive Cancel snooze control. Keep unknown factual values as an em dash; do not invent a subject amount.
- **Date:** today is the minimum. Until is inclusive; eligibility resumes only when until is earlier than the current London business date. Use the authoritative result for the date on which the item becomes eligible again rather than equating it with the chosen Until date or pay date.
- **Warning:** amber plain payroll wording, required acknowledgement only when the authoritative warning requires it, and disabled Apply until acknowledgement and valid inputs are present. Invalid dates receive field-level feedback. Preserve saved-rate/selection decisions according to their actual owner rather than the old modal's clearing text.
- **Refused/stale/conflict:** keep the form and entered values visible. Explain the authoritative reason, such as unsnoozing shifts before snoozing their Timesheet. Disable or permit retry according to that exact outcome; do not dismiss the window as if it succeeded.
- **Changed close:** use the standard adapter's asynchronous close interception. A changed form asks whether to discard changes, with the safe keep-editing choice first. This is separate from the Candidate Include selection confirmation.
- **Unsnooze / Cancel Snooze:** show one scoped confirmation, with the safe choice first. Use the supplied consequence that the item returns to Ready for the next Draft. Clear preserves financial facts and uses the current snooze identity/version.
- **All expenses:** freeze the complete supported current selection once across every page, with its exact scope signatures. One set/clear confirmation covers the complete frozen set. The result lists every success, stale/refused or failure; Retry failed only repeats unresolved failed work with the required idempotent identity and never repeats successful lines. Mileage is already a supported EXPENSE_LINE schema, not a reason to create a new producer.
- **Result:** show where the item is now visible and when it becomes eligible again; indefinite results direct the user to Payment Advances / Snoozes. Present the notice at the current window/surface, following T04f and SCR-35d. Do not transplant a legacy toast over another screen. Refresh exactly affected Candidate/detail/Timesheet views after the owned result, without polling or replacing authoritative money with local sums.
