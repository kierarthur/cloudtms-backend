## 4. Native units and source money qualifications

Immutable approved segments retain `hours_day/night/sat/sun/bh`, their original bucket pay rates and actual `approved_source_ex_vat` (1236:52–77). Captured segment parents retain actual A/R/H, positive INCLUDED/EXCLUDED, recovery and overhang (2223:1084–1105; migration1100:90–110). Therefore a partially selected or snoozed parent must use its original captured INCLUDED members, never all approved hours. `segment_attribution_complete` and the native parent conservation proof are prerequisites.

* **Untouched multibucket:** each retained INCLUDED segment has exact positive native bucket units. A MANUAL_REPLACEMENT_RATE can use those exact units even though other buckets exist. SUGGESTED_EQUIVALENT_BASIS also works when native source bucket contributions are actually qualified: frozen units/rates and component/segment source money must conserve. Do not refuse merely because `bucket_count>1`. Ordinary publication stores native segment `pay_amount` separately from bucket vectors (1001:388–418,451–468); seal proves segment/parent totals, not every bucket's money. Qualify the original native unit-price basis and its penny conservation instead of assuming every published amount equals a guessed bucket product. For Source, preserve the original bound book/receipt/head/classification qualification and original units/rates. A genuinely unbound Source bucket is a typed unavailable result; its presence is not a blanket excuse to reject ordinary or otherwise bound multibucket work.
* **Single-bucket partial:** first positively qualify exactly one native positive bucket/unit-price basis, the exact original SOURCE channel, original segment/component money versus its unit-price basis, and the captured A/R/H identity. A quotient alone does not establish those facts. Only then can all retained source A/R/H belong to that bucket: derive equivalent residual units directly from the exact included SOURCE amount and its immutable positive native rate, with finite/range and native source-penny conservation checks. Do not truncate equivalent units to six decimals merely because the original approved hours have that scale; use a qualified representation that reconstitutes the original included SOURCE pennies, or refuse that representation honestly. This is not residual/approved pro-rata or a target-money ratio. Keep approved worked units distinct from equivalent residual units in advice. Zero source rate, an independent lump amount or missing native source attribution cannot produce inferred units. A partially spent segment with multiple positive buckets lacks per-bucket R/H in current storage and cannot assign those debits by bucket order or proportion.
* **OR47 narrowed scope:** whole untouched INCLUDED segments retain their bucket vectors even when excluded siblings are snoozed/unselected/query-held. Fully excluded units/positive money contribute zero to target quote; negative recovery and held overhang remain in the original financial scope. A partially spent INCLUDED segment follows the same one-bucket/multibucket qualification above. Native equality must be against the completed captured included amount, not necessarily the parent's algebraic residual when positive and negative segments coexist.
* **MANUAL_AMOUNT:** retain exact saved original Position basis and included residual scope qualification (2100:302–309); never scale an old full amount over partial/snoozed/new-held scope. This mode belongs to positively amount-led code semantics; do not invent a rate or divide a component-wide manual amount among standard segments.
