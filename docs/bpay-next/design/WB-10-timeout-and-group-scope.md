## Existing group storage cannot replace exact group amounts

`private.bpay_next_workbench_group` core1252:33–63 stores native Work/Case identity, Candidate/client/channel/week/current revision, projection/financial/metadata revisions, complete/dirty/progress state and READY/ACTION/BLOCKED/WAITING/stale-snooze counters. Native1316 adds certified_zero. Current migrations add **no per-group source money totals or per-section unknown counters**. Candidate/scope summary money is at a different natural key and may combine many groups; it cannot be substituted as this group's amount. Reason counters cannot derive a money amount or prove exact zero.

Actual1601 CANDIDATE_GROUPS at427–440 bounds parent seed to10 then aggregates every current item of each selected group/section. Required outputs are min stored client label, first stored reason by priority/id, any deduction direction, any selectable row, unknown count (excluding actual certified-zero GROUP_EVIDENCE), READY GROSS_ADD included source, READY NET_ADD included source, GROSS_DEDUCT balance and NET_DEDUCT balance. Unknown count causes all corresponding public group money fields to remain NULL. The real `(group_key,...)` index makes the scan scoped; it does not cap child rows. No history is read, but one enormous group remains larger than one bounded child page.

Existing `private.bpay_next_workbench_group_contribution_v1(text)` (1252:33–84) already derives deterministic group contributions for WB08's natural summary delta owner. These rows are transient; they are not stored per group. Its deduction count is selectable deductions, while the current group reader uses any deduction direction, so that counter alone is also not equivalent. Its certified-zero exception and per-stage money expressions are reusable semantics, not a new calculator.

If stronger finite enrichment is required, the smallest genuine supporting projection is a fixed per-(group_key,section including ALL) read-summary of the exact above current item expressions, completed atomically at the **existing full refresh_work/refresh_case tail** where those owners already calculate final group counts after writing the complete item set. It need not introduce financial commands, command-clock cuts, headroom, money ownership or producer protocols. Preserve actual dirty/Processing barrier and unknown/zero semantics; no reader rebuild or truncation of child aggregates. Existing full Work final is1410 and Case final is1252 (registry must be freshly checked before any edit). Mark-processing/metadata-only jobs should preserve or mark this read projection unavailable, not revalue money or clear barriers.

That projection would require concrete schema and refresher support plus clean-launch/retained-group completion authority; it is **not already present** and should not be silently claimed as existing reuse. The WB10 card explicitly owns indexes/bounds/measurement and R2 bounded seed-before-enrichment; choose deliberately whether current scoped aggregate plus indexes remains an unmeasured performance dependency, or whether those exact supporting read-projection fields are necessary. Do not infer need for a broad WB08 money-body rewrite. Missing projection may never be fabricated as zero. An arbitrary group-size cap would violate the no-fixed-Timesheet-limit requirement.
