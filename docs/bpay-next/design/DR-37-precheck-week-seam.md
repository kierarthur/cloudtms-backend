## Native ordering, stale proof and commit admission

Original `claim_simple_paye_net_job_v1` final0110:42–45 rejects any same-Candidate earlier job with status<>DONE. Current public financial_job0016:960–961 still calls that helper for PAYE_NET_ENTRY, including Case NET. Retain this predicate for fresh/reclaimed original jobs. It is not just same-Worker ordering.

Current enroller2041:567–569 locks the original enrollment clock and admits **exact agency_sequence=last_enrolled_sequence+1**. PREPARE member enrollment advances that clock only after its original complete member delivery. Thus a NET job cannot be enrolled past an older unfinished PREPARE enrollment and then gain a late earlier Candidate job through the normal enroller. Current PREPARE claim2259:1101–1106 also rejects all earlier non-DONE Candidate jobs. Later cancellation/posting/expiry claims retain that same command-order condition. They cannot publish an earlier Run's contribution or run cancellation while this earlier NET job is nonterminal, even if its lease expires. Cancellation intake records intent; it is not proof that a later cleanup writer ran.

For the **current native ordered paths**, a completed metadata pass performed under this exact original NET job/command/module/lease plus original earlier-job-DONE admission remains valid against later publication/cancel jobs before fence acquisition. Bind the proof to that operation and preserve the original Candidate control active-owner epoch checks on every page. A clock/enrollment/command mismatch, unexpected earlier pending job or invalid original lease is infrastructure/stale ownership, never proof from a new/latest job and never a larger-revision unlock. Same-job fresh lease takeover must retain exact membership and the native ordering admission, not blindly trust a proof from another command/module.

At final validation retain the captured week revision and exact earlier contribution identities. Actual changes to existing contribution/floor amounts increment native worker_week.period_revision; the business rule still concerns only prior rows, not whole-week unresolved count. Future owning-card writers must keep the command order and Candidate fence calls. A new caller that can publish eligible Worker/contribution state outside that order would invalidate the lease/order proof and requires **a second bounded metadata pass under the fence immediately before COMMITTING**, plus an exact fence/order assertion on that writer. Reuse/reset the same cursor/complete fields for that pass; do not add a framework, producer flag, all-prior array or unbounded final EXISTS. Once destructive commit has begun, mismatches remain invariant/resumable failures and cannot become late business REFUSED.
