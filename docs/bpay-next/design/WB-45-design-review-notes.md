## Smallest complete store and lookup

The proposed two named private tables and one compatible lookup are appropriate. A Work/component-only key is insufficient: original physical/economic basis, native source revision/line, rate family and exact bucket/code/signature must distinguish DAY/NIGHT and arbitrary Additional codes. Keep original SOURCE amount/units/classification separate from selected target channel, actual Umbrella identity and immutable policy/date operands. Unknown historical identity remains NULL and cannot qualify an identity-dependent decision by coalescing to a current setting.

Versioned decision economics are immutable. An exact saved version remains readable after clear/replacement and must not be rewritten by later published revision or target changes. Append-only need/save/clear events retain actor, original request/command association and the exact version affected. The event/decision relationship must prevent cross-Work/version clears and duplicate original-request effects. A guarded ACTIVE-to-superseded/cleared lifecycle field is acceptable only as an indexable current-state projection of those immutable events; it must not mutate stored money/basis. Alternatively derive current state from indexed latest events. Do not add a second state/history store solely to implement this choice.

Define one ACTIVE version for the full compatible identity, with NULL-safe uniqueness for genuinely nullable identity fields. The lookup must reject more than one qualifying version rather than pick latest by timestamp. Based-on and published revision mapping must be explicit and own the same Work/approved component. A mapped published revision must be complete, immutable and independently compatible with the original physical/economic/bucket/source tuple; revision equality alone is insufficient. Mapping may not make unrelated later revisions inherit treatment. Same original request/version replays exactly; changed input at that identity refuses.

Use existing private owner/RLS/revoke conventions and bounded typed text/JSON. No browser role/service direct table access, public trigger, producer changes, new resolution mode, new command or generic proof/digest framework is needed here. Future save/clear owner must serialize the exact Work/native command boundary and append its event atomically; WB45 does not build that command early.

## Essential residual contract conditions

The shared residual quote must derive authority from exact retained native scope, not accept browser-derived remaining money/hours or a saved full quote as allocation evidence. Core1324 `bpay_next_rate_detail:240–254` stores immutable bucket hours/rate; `bpay_next_rate_schedule:262–276` stores separate available rate-family/code evidence. These are not paid bucket allocations. Segment1100 `bpay_next_run_work_segment_parent:90–110` and origin/effect shares114–145 preserve segment source/target conservation, but their money shares do not in themselves identify how a mixed DAY/NIGHT segment was partially paid or held.

Consequently a scalar Position/component residual cannot prove remaining mixed-bucket units. Full untouched exact bucket or exact zero may be qualified from native facts; partial mixed attribution requires actual owned bucket/unit evidence. Missing or multiple incompatible attribution must return the named typed ambiguity/evidence gap, never a guessed proportion, rate-derived blended split, copied full target or arbitrary allocation priority. Do not create a new attribution ledger/producer rewrite just to force a quote in this card. The genuine £160/£120/£40 case must be demonstrated by its native provable basis; an ambiguous instance is a required refusal, not fabricated acceptance.

Manual replacement rate consumes only proved remaining target units. MANUAL_AMOUNT is permitted only for actual amount-led owner semantics and cannot be proportionally scaled onto a partial residual without an exact retained attribution. Missing source used rate/quantity or charge evidence does not itself prove an amount-led component. Reuse the original mode/classification guards; keep reimbursement fixed and finance Case restructure with FC. Explicit zero and required manual reason remain distinct from missing operands.

Only after identity, mode, residual and native immutable policy qualification may the helper call unchanged `private.pay_finance_selected_quote_v1` (1157:10–86). Its53–59 arithmetic uses the caller's target units/manual amount and does not establish residual attribution. No alternative converter or ratio calculator is authorised. Future consumption must preserve current1419 exact run-line/ACTIVE hold/Position/worker-total equality and all current Source/OR47/money locks; no decision leaves the existing valuer route unchanged.
