## Native cash authority and lifecycle

The mutually exclusive original-obligation and paged-original-leg modes satisfy the card without a duplicate counter, an unbounded total or browser arithmetic. A partial destination group cannot execute any original leg:2025 requires COMPLETE, full created/sealed counts and conserved vector before execution. Before that point the exact original projection cash is counted once, and emitted partial legs carry NULL counted reservation with the explicit included-in-parent state. Once materialisation is complete, header reservation cash becomes explicitly paged/NULL and each exact original leg carries its own native reservation. Accepted projection information remains separate and is never added to those legs.

The distinct ordinary Umbrella branch is necessary and accurately matches final2018 begin eligibility. Its first legal projection is created only at transfer begin; confirmed pre-build0/0 can use the qualified frozen gross obligation, and legal post-build request0/projection1 must not be mistaken for a PAYE replacement request. Materialised scope always uses the original transfer/group's exact retained projection, including a retired projection, rather than current worker pointers. No-group pending PAYE replacement is explicit pending/NULL until its true accepted basis completes.

Case-only0-WORK and positive Case payout are supported through the original completed DRAFT/NET state and projection. Destination-state absence legitimately means a single Candidate obligation; present vectors require actual completed identity/conservation. OWN and external destination amounts are disjoint. Exact group-leg/destination links qualify each transfer cash; no per-line or per-Client allocation is needed. Reissue is excluded from original Draft reservation and retains separate returned-cash lineage.

The proposed precedence correctly excludes cash at actual SETTLED intake, before asynchronous member posting releases every hold. Final2018 inserts outcome/request and changes SETTLED atomically. Current2025 posted-leg evidence proves later posting, not another cash amount. RETURNED does not restore the original Draft reservation. INTERNAL_ZERO preserves genuine zero cash and pending positive source obligations, with actual internal receipt/request identity and partial-group completion kept separate. ISSUED_CSV remains an outstanding held reservation awaiting a recorded settlement, not proof of physically unpaid bank cash. Execution-uncertain states are NULL, not optimistic remaining cash or invented zero.

Cancellation REQUESTED is not the native cancellation fence. Group2102 first checks all original legs while intent stays REQUESTED;0460 then establishes whole-worker/binding/request CANCELLING before paged releases or partial final leg markings. The proposed exact binding/group/checkpoint tuple therefore supplies zero non-executable original reservation during genuine cancellation, while retained active holds are releasing capacity. Simple0260 similarly fences worker/transfer/request atomically. Blocked intent leaves reservation intact; a bare terminal-looking status without the native scope/receipt is conflict rather than automatic zero.

## Holds, scopes and bounded read

Actual active captured WORK/Case holds support the original reservation; their source/gross amounts are not summed into a Candidate NET share. Per-materialised-leg hold evidence is restricted to that leg's immutable members and actual allocation/instruction scope. Certified-zero WORK and zero Case results legitimately have no hold, so an all-rows-have-holds requirement would be wrong. A positive supposedly unexecuted leg without original reservation authority is refused. Cash receipt/cancellation evidence takes precedence over active holds.

The design retains exact run/worker/Candidate scope and existing service/admin/module channel authority. The helper is private STABLE and write-free, with the STATUS integration likewise STABLE. It must not call2025 execution from a reader: that routine takes bank FOR SHARE and imposes execution readiness. No bank data, new command, money owner, producer/PIN or Rule-B change is justified.

One SQL invocation gives a consistent header/row snapshot. Different keyset pages remain live reads; mode changes replace the current reservation presentation instead of concatenating parent and leg cash. Existing100-transfer paging and encoded envelope bounds remain. The design truthfully distinguishes this row bound from a potentially proportional member/hold EXISTS check and from database statement lifetime; it promises no unmeasured100-row CPU or8-second PostgreSQL completion guarantee.
