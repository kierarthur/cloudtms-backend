## Open concrete findings

### F25-S01 — P1: original-command receipts cannot retain multiple individual events

`bpay_next_input_version_receipt.command_id` is UNIQUE (line 329). The protocol preserves every individual OLD/NEW event and reuses an already-ordered Source/financial publication's original command, instead of allocating a second metadata command. Multiple genuine gate events sharing that original completion cannot each acquire their mandatory deferred receipt: the second event violates this unique constraint and rolls back the originating transaction. Preserve event_id uniqueness and exact sequence/epoch binding; permit many event receipts under a genuine original command. The future INPUT_VERSION owner/enroller can separately enforce its one-event zero-member metadata contract. Do not collapse individual events or allocate substitute financial commands to satisfy the schema.

### F25-S02 — P1: accepted case rows can substitute another selection revision or contradict NO_CASE

The case child FK `(accepted_input_id,run_id,candidate_id)` (lines 225–226) and its independent selection-item FK (227–228) do not require its `selection_revision` to equal the revision retained by the accepted parent. A NO_CASE parent with NULL revision can therefore own a valid old case selection item; a parent fixed at revision 1 can own revision 2. Add an exact parent key/FK including `case_selection_revision`/child selection_revision, or an equally explicit INSERT authority guard installed with the table. Preserve selected=false items where they belong to the exact retained selection. Later live selection rows cannot redefine the accepted scope.

### F25-S03 — P1: publication and effective-cut provenance can refer to another valid WORK

Accepted WORK checks only publication_command_id existence plus command/sequence (lines 110–113), independently of its WORK/effective revision/Candidate. The already available UNIQUE `(command_id,work_id,revision_id,candidate_id)` on publication (migration 2010:19) supplies an exact composite FK; use it. Effective cut independently permits another publication_command_id/predecessor_sequence and selected_line_count than its accepted-work parent (lines 189–194). Bind those immutable fields to that parent or explicitly refuse mismatch at the INSERT boundary. Otherwise all current individual FKs pass while the retained predecessor is unrelated to the effective WORK. The future resolver still must prove predecessor sequence is below the exact PREPARE sequence, genuine APPLIED/DONE completion, correct PREPARE job/lease and complete member count; this schema review does not substitute for those owner checks.

### F25-S04 — P1: weekly floor evidence has no same-Candidate/week or contribution-worker identity

The weekly child references accepted_input, contribution_id, original_run_worker_id and transfer/return IDs independently (lines 239 onward). It can retain another Candidate's contribution, pair it with an unrelated valid original worker, or import a different week into this Candidate's floor. Carry/bind the immutable Candidate/week and contribution→original worker identity with exact existing identity keys or a typed INSERT guard. Retained contribution_revision and amount remain captured facts; do not FK their values to later mutable live counters or create a second finance ledger. Actual source contribution has Candidate/week/worker and contextual transfer/return relationships at `04102026_0300...sql:611–646`; reuse that ownership pattern. The accepted original dates/times should also preserve the source's finite date/time shape.

### F25-S05 — P2: receipt/accepted epoch and version-owner identities need exact binding

Command sequence FKs omit module_epoch, allowing a receipt or accepted_input with a different positive epoch from its exact command. Candidate/Umbrella/policy version-event FKs bind only event_id, so an ORDERED_VERSION accepted Candidate can name another Candidate's valid event; the event/typed child input_kind/owner identity is also not mutually constrained. Bind immutable command epoch and typed owner identity, or install exact INSERT guards with the evidence tables. Applicable version receipt sequence must be before the accepted PREPARE; baseline fields are actual at-acceptance facts, not historical commands. The deferred event→receipt FK already enforces event/receipt epoch equality but does not establish these other relationships.
