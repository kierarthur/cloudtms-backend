## Meaning of the separate business date

The card requires each supported payment-transition fact to retain server-owned ingestion and its distinct business occurrence, atomically with the state change. `outcome.received_at_utc` is ingestion; `financial_effect.occurred_at_utc` is economic posting and cannot become a bank date. Approved PSP-15 in both R3 check and addendum Payment Summary policy is explicit: paid date=SETTLED outcome occurrence, return date=RETURNED outcome occurrence; instruction issue/run confirmation retain their own meanings; received time belongs only in History. PSP-18 requires UK local date/time display while UTC storage remains unchanged.

A generated **stored** native DATE from `(occurred_at_utc AT TIME ZONE 'Europe/London')::date` on each actual immutable outcome/internal receipt is a minimal consistent implementation. The fixed zone avoids session-TimeZone dependence and handles the British calendar day. It is the calendar projection of the already admitted immutable occurrence, not a newly independently supplied bank-date operand. No new API/command date input or mutable-workflow reconstruction is needed. The original occurrence and ingestion fields remain separately stored. Internal receipt date is the genuine zero-cash accounting occurrence day, never a bank paid date. Deterministic derivation from retained historical occurrence preserves an actual fact; it does not invent an event from ingestion or completion. No current accepted path needs a new producer.
