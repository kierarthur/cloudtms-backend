Current Worker considers INGESTED/DUPLICATE/IGNORED/UNMATCHED_REVIEW_REQUIRED/FAILED_FINAL final **ingestion** statuses; the last two still require census review, and successful ingestion can still represent unknown cash. Do not reuse `isReceiptFullyProcessed` as financial completion.
