## COMMON_HEAD and pair publication proof

The accepted-origin helper reads the exact immutable receipt named by the retained binding. It checks receipt/binding candidate, bundle revision and request digest; one or two retained members; exact member numbers and membership cardinality. Every member is checked against the receipt's same ordinal HEAD/root/family/version arrays and retained predecessor HEAD. HEAD candidate, committed evidence, publication digest, bundle identity and transaction token remain required.

Every retained member must resolve exactly one original WORK/revision/publication tuple matching one of the binding's stored publication triples, with exact predecessor revision. It requires original APPLIED/DONE publication, sealed COMPLETE POSITION_APPLY command, completed membership count, sequence before acceptance, matching module epoch, and DONE/DONE job with cleared lease. It does not substitute today's HEAD state, WORK current/applied revision, position applied revision, current binding pointer, or a new publication command. Receipt and binding immutable schema constraints supply the original one/two-member shape. The helper does not add two-command or cross-channel payment execution; existing supported capture scope remains a separate caller restriction.
