Enforce the native issued/final assembled response bounds, or reuse a narrow safe key transport encoding preserving the full text/C-order value. Do not trim labels or fabricate completion. Keep any honest row-too-large refusal qualified to this reader. Office should enforce its new request envelope before dispatch rather than sending a known oversized GET.
