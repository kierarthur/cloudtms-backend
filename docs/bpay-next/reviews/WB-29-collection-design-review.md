The publication split is necessary and sufficiently narrow. Reconcile's original :798–812 simultaneously binds a Source-book publication and the current apply command. Observation retains its own QUEUED/APPLYING/APPLIED status contract rather than borrowing reconcile's later-owner APPLIED/DONE contract.

Root line joins, when needed by Source bindings, are additional exact same-component authority; they cannot replace those paid anchors. The explicit REMOVED classification and receipt/head/authorisation proof, genuine certified zero, withdrawal retention and NULL tax/amount distinctions remain required. All segment and nonsegment R/W/H, principal, manual/automatic mode and free-recovery arithmetic stays native. The ordinary-only no-effect fallback is unchanged; this batch does not invent a Source paid anchor.
